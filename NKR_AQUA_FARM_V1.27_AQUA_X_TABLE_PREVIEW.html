Crop Performance — FCR & Survival

Calculated from saved stocking, feed and harvest records. Survival is estimated from harvested pieces (harvest kg × count/kg) divided by stocked pieces. For an unfinished crop, this is survival recorded to date, not a final survival result.

Backup & Restore

Your data is stored in this browser, not on GitHub. Export backups regularly. Avoid entering sensitive business data until you are comfortable with this storage method.

Keep the downloaded JSON file in Files or iCloud Drive. A download is not an automatic cloud backup.

Restoring replaces browser records. Download a fresh backup first.

Clear Test Transactions

Deletes stocking, feed, trial net, expenses, and harvest test records from this browser. All sites and pond names remain unchanged.

Download and keep your JSON backup before clearing. This cannot be undone except by restoring a backup.
